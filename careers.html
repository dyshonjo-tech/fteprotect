Personnel Standards

What We Look For.

Depending on assignment and jurisdiction, candidates may be expected to possess appropriate:

  • Security licensing
  • Personal-protection credentials
  • Private-investigator credentials
  • Driver's license, clean or acceptable driving record
  • Firearms qualifications where applicable
  • Medical training
  • Defensive-driving training
  • Executive-protection education
  • Communication skills
  • Report-writing capability

Licensing and qualification requirements will vary according to assignment.
